# Reference notes

These are newly written production notes, not the owner's missing supporting package. The selected western PNGs were not found locally. The written direction remains authoritative; generated comparison boards in this branch are provisional targets, not substitutes for owner approval.

- [OpenAI — Building games with Astra](https://developers.openai.com/blog/how-to-build-games-with-astra): the described workflow separates player-experience constraints, visual targets, architecture and empirical iteration. Applied here as independent simulation/renderer layers and repeated actual-gameplay comparisons. Its WebGPU space-game example does not prove this game's mobile performance.
- [Capcom — Street Fighter6 control types](https://www.capcom-support.com/hc/en-us/articles/7122030981660-What-are-the-control-types-in-Street-Fighter-6): accessible special inputs can coexist with a fuller fighting system. Our design inference is to keep direct strike/element/guard inputs while retaining spacing, commitment and recovery. We are not copying Capcom move lists or balance rules.
- [Nintendo — Mario Kart8 Deluxe](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Games/Nintendo-Switch-games/Mario-Kart-8-Deluxe-1173281.html): beginner steering support and readable opportunities for reversals inform accessibility. Our bounded road and automatic running reduce input load; animal running, authored hazards and scoring are original. No Nintendo artwork or track layouts are used.
- [Bandai Namco — Tekken8](https://en.bandainamcoent.eu/tekken/tekken-8): visual-presentation reference supplied by the commission. Its promotional page is not collision-frame data or a quantitative target for our phone renderer. The commission's requested anticipation, contact and weight still require direct observation in Animal Racers.

Accessed2026-09-16. No commercial game assets were downloaded into the runtime.
