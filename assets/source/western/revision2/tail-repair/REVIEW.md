This is an isolated six-form repair candidate. Canonical `src/**`, `public/**` and the six canonical Blender files remain byte-frozen to the inputs recorded in `freeze.json` from commit54ecd6e. It has not been promoted. Full cinematic and owner acceptance remain open.

## Why the previous review failed

Normal online side-view evidence in `scripts/network/browser-revision2/run-2026-09-20T02-57-20-975Z/01-lion-wolf-round1-guest-reply-1.png` exposed a floating Lion shaft and a detached Wolf tuft. The Unicorn failure was subsequently confirmed. Earlier front/three-quarter material and contact checks had missed these defects; whole-model bounds and fixed limb lengths could not detect them. Leaf2.2 A2 was correctly reopened.

The source had two independent attachment faults. The tail was a separate shaft with no shared rump boundary, with its upright rest root already behind the body. More severely, the four Revision2-authored upright clips reused the same mutable `current` Vector for each tail head, then mutated it with `+=`. All stored heads moved to the final tip. In the frozen Lion fight_idle, tail_0 began at Blender(y1.08,z.67), rather than its native(y.36,z.95), and its direction was reversed. Preserving those malformed rotations during a translation-only repair reproduced large stretching.
