## Contact experiment and integration review

The camera exposed the existing muzzle overlap clearly. Cycle 5 measured idle forward head extents of .862/.970/1.030 m for Lion/Wolf/Unicorn, incompatible with .85 m body separation. Increasing separation alone would leave the low strike pose short. An isolated source experiment moved the head/neck attachment .30m backward and reduced normal-strike lean while retaining face identity, paw reach, fixed limb lengths and cup contact. Its first version detached the upper Unicorn mane and rotated Wolf's collar onto the cheek. That rejected version is retained. The repaired v2b reshapes/reweights those attachments while preserving body and action channels. Source review and both independent runtime oracles pass; the parent also reran the distorted-arm negative control, which failed as intended.

The rules review also found a mirrored-role counterexample during a jumping cross-up and Lion lunge. Shared pre-step positions now determine new attack facing; reachable exact-X landings use prior orientation instead of slot priority. Three meaningful regressions fail on their defective predecessors and pass after repair. The parent independently reran all38 simulation cases. The camera-only recording above predates these repairs.

The loopback-only [contact viewer](../../../scripts/qa/README.md) uses the actual runtime renderer with v2b GLBs. It holds authored poses; it does not step the simulation or establish normal-control gameplay. Every PNG in `contact-inspection/` has its selected actions, gap/time, named source hashes, three candidate GLB hashes and PNG hash in adjacent JSON. The UI was operated through normal browser controls, without page-state injection.

- [Nine pairings at1.75m/.21s](contact-inspection/nine-strikes-1.75m.jpg): head silhouettes remain separate, and the striking forelimb visibly reaches the rival's lowered forelimbs. This addresses the previous gross overlap, without claiming exact 3D mesh intersection or all pose combinations.
- [Nine pairings at1.90m/.18s](contact-inspection/nine-strikes-range-boundary.jpg): the first active frame at proposed maximum range exposes a small visible Unicorn-to-Lion/Unicorn gap. Additional saved1.85m Unicorn pairings reduce that gap. Canonical integration therefore uses the conservative normal reach1.85m, CPU trigger1.82m and pursuit1.80m, with body separation1.75m. The final40-case rules suite and current policy audit subsequently passed for these values.
- A first wire-edge Unicorn pulse read like a debug cage in runtime and was rejected. The revised translucent flare, limited to the special's active interval, provides a visible reach for the braced-hoof special. Normal attacks remain hoof strikes. The active rematch below subsequently shows the integrated1.95m special flare during actual combat.

A further animation review found that the guard clip raises the forelimbs once, but the renderer repeated that clip every .6 s while defense stayed held. Guard now uses the authoritative elapsed time and clamps at its raised endpoint, like other one-shot actions. The local viewer confirms a raised pose at2.4s; a physical held-defense gameplay replay remains required. The camera-only capture above predates this change.
