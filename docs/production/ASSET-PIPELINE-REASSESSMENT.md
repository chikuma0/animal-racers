# Asset pipeline reassessment after the Lion studies

2026-09-16. Internal alpha, no visual acceptance. This is a decision record, not a claim that an alternative asset has been integrated.

The isolated hero-art-v1 study improved orbital recesses and continuous muzzle planes, but repeatedly missed the mane target. Long orange locks read as human hair; later radial locks read as regular swollen strips around a smooth shell. The underlying annular surface and repeated loft profiles need reconstruction, not another orientation or density adjustment. Accessory intrusion into the smaller upright muzzle also needed a separate correction. Root rejected the studies before full motion rendering or canonical promotion. Their editable sources and rejected previews remain in the experiment folder.

## Investigated alternatives

- [Zee's Lion Animated](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lion-animated-d30f4862f2464284b157e377e6540d6c): publisher lists CC Attribution,10.4k triangles and26 animations. Actual embedded preview was inspected and showed a plain untextured feline without a mane. It does not resolve the observed mane/material quality problem. No download or integration.
- [doizy's Lion](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lion-79d5e1173bba4f2b80661620a2eca9bc): publisher lists CC Attribution,5.5k triangles/3.9k vertices. Actual embedded preview shows much richer mane and muzzle detail, with a realistic painted appearance. Download opens a sign-in requirement. No account was created or signed in, and no mesh was obtained. Full topology, textures, rig, original-source availability, adaptation cost and identity across both stances remain unverified; preview alone cannot select this as the pipeline.
- [NewDLC's3d wolf](https://opengameart.org/content/3d-wolf): primary page lists CC0 and provides a direct Blender archive. The archive was downloaded for isolated inspection outside the repository. Blender4.5.9 with auto-execution disabled read one2,923-vertex mesh,36-bone armature and no animation actions. Color/normal/roughness/specular images are included. The completed isolated assessment rejects direct adoption: cartoon face/fur and raised-shoulder stretch fail the target. Its immutable source, 37-frame probe, three stills and provenance are retained under `assets/source/western/licensed-wolf-review/` as reference only; no runtime integration.
- [TC5051's Full Rig Lion2](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/full-rig-lion-2-90d5a85e8e6e4ea9b0ac8628ce9041f5) explicitly says its lion came from Cadnav. That indirect provenance has not established the original mesh's redistribution rights; excluded from current candidates.

## Local reconstruction feasibility

[Official TripoSR](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR) documents an MIT release covering code/weights and a typical6GB VRAM inference footprint. It produces static reconstructed geometry; rigging, topology cleanup and full dual-stance motion would still need inspection and authoring. The [actual current entry point](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VAST-AI-Research/TripoSR/main/run.py) forces CPU if CUDA is absent, so passing `--device mps` alone would not establish Apple GPU operation. A local experiment would need an isolated environment and explicit device-path validation. No model/weights have been installed, no image uploaded, and no inference-quality claim is made. Host contention currently makes adding a multi-GB inference workload inappropriate.

[Hunyuan3D2's current license](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2/main/LICENSE) includes territorial restrictions that also refer to outputs. It is not selected for an unrestricted public game asset workflow. No weights or code installed.

No3D generation connector was found in the enabled tool inventory. Blender4.5.9 is the actual available authoring tool. There was no Unity/Unreal or image-to-3D integration by implication.

Network Cycle8 is now locally complete and the Wolf assessment has been independently reverified. Next concrete action: a bounded Mast head-surface experiment with actual topology, reduced-cost render, deformation and two-form fit evidence. If Sketchfab access is needed after that assessment, request only that access with the specific candidate and reason. Do not promote any preview-only or uninspected mesh, and do not call the existing characters cinematic.

## Additional editable Lion candidate

[Djsedj’s Mast](https://opengameart.org/content/mast-blender-425) is offered by its author under CC0 as a Blender4.2.5 source. The actual publisher preview was inspected: cheeks, nasal bridge and orbital forms are connected and materially richer than the rejected procedural face; its long swept mane remains hairlike, so the preview does not establish our mane target. The publisher discloses broken UVs and a very high subdivided count; those are adaptation work, not acceptable runtime defaults. Root downloaded only its474.8KiB archive to isolated local research storage (SHA256 `19667562d118d6efedd9e60361100a289bb4f004d3c6899ca559ebe7503fc37b`), containing `Mast.blend` (3,250,356bytes). The initial source inventory below is complete; detailed head topology, deformation and reduced-cost visual fit remain to be assessed with auto-execution disabled. No candidate is integrated.

The official [Quaternius animal pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html) provides CC0 editable animated animals, but the listed roster does not solve the Lion mane requirement. No pack was downloaded or adopted solely for convenience.

Mast’s reference to inherited Aedan UVs was traced to [Aedan by the same author](https://opengameart.org/content/aedan-blend), also offered as CC0. That removes the unexplained third-party attribution gap from this candidate’s stated origin, while actual file/topology inspection is still required.

Actual Mast source read completed in Blender4.5.9 with `--factory-startup --disable-autoexec`: main mesh4,379vertices/4,238polygons, separate mane1,437vertices/1,008polygons, one shared material, 210 rig bones including face/jaw/eyelid/mane controls, no authored animation actions and no texture images. Subdivision level2 is active on both major meshes; mane also has Solidify. The unmodified source SHA256 is `e3c29d87557d29ca70b700b1861149bf6111711f065c5d5c2379427630441838`. A future head-only study should evaluate reduced subdivision and a small facial/mane control subset before any two-form transfer; the current full source is not a phone-ready replacement. No source bytes were changed.
